Giving Without Knowing

What Type of Donor Are You?

Ten questions, about two minutes.

Whether you give to candidates, political causes, or issue advocacy, we all have a style. You may not know yours yet.

This is about how you decide, not how much you give. The same four patterns show up whether the gift is $50 or $5,000.

Drawn from a Duke MPA capstone framework on how donors decide. No answers are collected or sent anywhere; this page keeps your results to itself.

Framework: four decision axes (how opportunities arrive, how they're judged, how money is allocated, who influences) from qualitative research on donor decision-making.
